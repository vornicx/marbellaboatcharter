Curated experiences

The charter becomes memorable when the day has a point.

The business already offers much more than basic yacht hire: luxury cruising, sailing, fishing, group events, Gibraltar, Morocco, corporate formats and celebrations. This page reframes those offers as experiences worth choosing, not menu items worth skimming.

01

Coastal escape

Swim, anchor, drift and return without rushing.

A short-to-medium charter for couples, families or friends who want sea time, swimming and an easy rhythm. This is the most natural entry point for Puerto Banús and Marbella departures.

  • Best duration2–4 hours
  • Ideal vesselsMotor yachts · catamaran
  • Why it worksEasy luxury without over-planning
02

Sunset charter

Shorter, more visual, perfectly timed.

A premium sunset trip is less about distance and more about departure timing, onboard atmosphere and the quality of the boat for late-afternoon light, drinks and music.

  • Best duration2–3 hours
  • Ideal vesselsElegant motor yacht
  • Why it worksHigh emotional value, low complexity
03

Corporate & groups

Larger parties still need polish, not chaos.

For corporate events, celebrations or larger groups, the planning challenge is coordination. The operator’s wider range — including larger vessels and multiple-boat formats — makes this a meaningful part of the business.

  • Best duration4–8 hours
  • Ideal vesselsGroup boats · multiple yachts
  • Why it worksHeadcount, transfers and catering can be organised properly
04

Fishing day

A specialist format, not an afterthought.

Fishing charters speak to a different client need. They should therefore be positioned clearly, with the right boats and language, rather than buried under generic “activities”.

  • Best duration4–10 hours
  • Ideal vesselsFishing boats
  • Why it worksPurpose-built experience on the water
05

Further routes

Gibraltar, Sotogrande and selected Morocco itineraries.

Longer routes should feel aspirational and serious. They change the discussion from a simple “how much is the boat?” to “what vessel, what weather window and what route logic make sense?”.

  • Best durationLonger day or special itinerary
  • Ideal vesselsLarger premium motor yachts
  • Why it worksTransforms charter into a destination experience

Support services

Catering and yacht management make the brand feel more established.

Those services matter because they suggest Marbella Boat Charter is not merely moving passengers onto a boat — it is supporting a wider yachting ecosystem.
