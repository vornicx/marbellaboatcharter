Current storage behaviour

The current public platform is designed to operate without advertising or analytics cookies by default. It uses browser storage for functional features such as a locally saved yacht shortlist. That shortlist stays on the user’s device unless the user later submits an enquiry.

Strictly necessary technology

Hosting, security and server infrastructure may use strictly necessary technical mechanisms to deliver requests safely, prevent abuse and maintain session integrity. These do not require consent where they are strictly necessary for the service requested.

Analytics and marketing

Non-essential analytics, advertising or profiling technologies should not be enabled until the platform has a consent mechanism that allows users to accept or refuse them before they are set. If such tools are introduced, this policy and the consent controls must be updated with the actual providers, purposes and retention periods.

Browser controls

You can clear local storage and cookies through your browser settings. Clearing site data will also remove locally saved yacht favourites on that device.
