# Marbella Boat Charter Platform — Level 5 scope

This repository is no longer treated as a brochure website. The target product is a premium charter platform with a public luxury experience and an operational layer for the Marbella Boat Charter team and participating yacht owners.

## Public product

- Luxury homepage and brand experience
- Complete fleet discovery by vessel type, guests, length, duration, use case and indicative price
- Individual yacht pages with real vessel-specific pricing, inclusions, exclusions, crew, toys, routes and enquiry context
- Advanced charter planner with party size, date, duration, departure area, preferred experience, catering/extras and vessel recommendation
- Experience pages for coastal cruising, sunset, dolphins, sailing, fishing, celebrations, corporate/groups and longer routes
- Destination pages for Puerto Banús, Marbella, Estepona, Sotogrande, Benalmádena and longer routes such as Gibraltar / selected Morocco itineraries when operationally available
- Catering and event support
- Yacht management / maintenance / charter-commercialisation acquisition for owners
- About, contact, booking terms, privacy, cookies and legal information
- EN / ES / FR information architecture

## Search / SEO / GEO

- Unique page titles, descriptions, canonicals and social metadata
- Human-readable URLs and preservation of high-value legacy URLs through redirects
- Vessel, service, destination and editorial internal-link architecture
- Structured data based on visible first-party facts; no fabricated ratings or availability
- XML sitemaps split by page family when the route count warrants it
- robots.txt and llms.txt discovery files
- Fact-rich sections that answer common charter questions without keyword stuffing
- Location-specific content that distinguishes the Puerto Banús base from the wider Costa del Sol operating area
- Strong entity consistency for business name, telephone, email and address
- Editorial content designed around first-party operational expertise, not generic generated travel copy

## Marbella Boat Charter Control

- Lead inbox and qualification
- Quotes and quote-line items
- Reservation calendar and operational statuses
- Deposits, balances and payment status
- Client CRM and visit / charter history
- Fleet inventory and vessel-specific commercial rules
- Availability blocks and owner-use blocks
- Owners and owner portal access
- Crew assignments and operational notes
- Maintenance tasks, documents and compliance records
- Catering and extras
- Content, SEO and redirects
- Reporting, audit trail and settings

## Owner portal

A secure owner-facing workspace is part of the platform direction. Owners should be able to see only their own vessels and authorised information: charter calendar, owner-use blocks, revenue statements, maintenance, documents, enquiries/performance and approved marketing assets.

## Quality rule

No production UI may rely on fake availability, fake reviews, fake booking confirmation or representative imagery presented as a real vessel. Public prices remain indicative until validated against the operator's source of truth. Any integration that is not connected must be presented as such internally rather than simulated to a customer.
