Booking & charter preparation

Clear terms are part of a premium booking experience.

The information below explains the commercial and operational principles currently communicated by Marbella Boat Charter. The vessel-specific quote and written booking confirmation remain the source of truth for each charter.

Reservation

Deposit secures the booking.

The operator’s published booking information states a 50% deposit to reserve the charter, with the remaining balance due seven days before the charter. Your written quote / confirmation should state the exact amounts and dates.

Weather

Safety comes before the programme.

Sea and weather conditions can affect the route or whether a charter can operate. The team monitors conditions and communicates operational decisions before departure. Any weather-related refund or rescheduling position must follow the written terms for the specific booking.

Catering

Order early enough to execute well.

Catering requests should be made at least 48 hours in advance according to the operator’s published information. Availability, menu, dietary requirements and cost must be confirmed for the specific vessel and date.

Before you pay

Your quote should make these points explicit.

  • VesselExact yacht / boat and charter date
  • DurationStart time, end time and boarding point
  • PriceSubtotal, VAT treatment and total
  • FuelIncluded allowance or additional-use rule
  • IncludedCrew, drinks, toys or other vessel-specific inclusions
  • Not includedMoorings, catering, transfers or other extras when applicable
  • PaymentsDeposit, balance and due dates
  • Cancellation / weatherThe written conditions applying to this charter

Important

There is no single inclusion rule that fits every vessel.

Fuel, drinks, crew, toys, moorings, VAT and extras differ between published yacht offers. The platform is being designed around vessel-specific commercial data so a client sees the relevant conditions instead of a misleading generic statement.

Need clarification?

Ask before the charter, not at the boarding point.

If any price, inclusion, route or payment condition is unclear, contact the charter desk and request written confirmation.
