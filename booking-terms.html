Reservation and payment

A reservation is currently confirmed by a 50% deposit, with the remaining 50% due at least seven days before the charter. The company’s published terms state that payment may be made by transfer, card or cash. The final quotation or booking confirmation should identify the amount, payment method and yacht-specific conditions.

Weather cancellation

Under the currently published policy, cancellation for weather conditions that prevent the charter is eligible for a complete refund. The operational decision is made with safety and the actual conditions in mind.

Technical cancellation

If the booked vessel suffers a technical failure or breakdown, the current policy is to seek a similar replacement vessel or provide a complete refund.

Yacht-specific terms

Published fleet prices and inclusions vary by vessel. VAT, fuel allowance, additional fuel, mooring at other ports, catering, watersports and crew composition may differ. The written quotation for the selected yacht is the source of truth for that charter.

Safety, routes and sea conditions

Routes, swimming, watersports and offshore passages remain subject to the skipper’s safety judgement, weather, sea conditions, marina requirements and any applicable legal or operational restrictions. A planned itinerary may need to change when safety requires it.
