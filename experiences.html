Experiences · Routes · Groups

A boat is only useful once the day has a shape.

The coast supports short sunset trips, swimming, sailing, fishing, group programmes and longer-distance itineraries. Duration should follow the plan, not the other way around.

01 · Coast & swim

Two hours gives you coastline. Four gives you time to stop.

A short charter works for a first outing or sunset. A four-hour window is the stronger choice when swimming, music and time at anchor are part of the plan.

  • Typical fitCouples, families, friends
  • Duration2–4 hours
  • DeparturePuerto Banús

02 · Sunset

Leave late. Keep the route simple.

For the last light, speed and distance are secondary. The useful decisions are departure time, boat shape, music, drinks and whether you want a swim before the sun drops.

Ask about sunset times

03 · Groups & corporate

One group can move as one programme, even across several boats.

For larger groups, the operator can coordinate multiple yachts, team activities and fishing formats. The public corporate programme describes simultaneous departures, grouped routes and four-hour activity formats.

  • FormatsMultiple yachts · fishing teams · sailing activity
  • Useful starting point4 hours
  • Planning inputHeadcount, objective, food/drink, transfer timing

04 · Sailing

Monohull for feel. Catamaran for space and stability.

The choice is functional. A monohull gives a more direct sailing experience; a catamaran is the better tool when deck area and steadiness matter more.

05 · Longer routes

Sotogrande, Gibraltar and selected Morocco itineraries need the right boat and enough time.

Distance changes fuel, duration, sea-state exposure and mooring requirements. These routes should begin as a tailored request, not a generic checkout.
