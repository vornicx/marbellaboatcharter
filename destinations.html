Destinations

Puerto Banús is the base. The coastline decides the day.

The right route depends on departure point, vessel, charter duration, sea conditions and what you want from the day. The platform treats destinations as planning context rather than pretending every yacht serves every port in exactly the same way.

Puerto Banús

Primary base and premium departure point.

Ideal for clients staying around the marina, Golden Mile and Nueva Andalucía, with fast access to classic coastal charters.

Marbella

Coast, swimming and easy day-charter rhythm.

A natural setting for private cruising, family charters, swimming stops and sunset programmes.
