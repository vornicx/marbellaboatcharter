About Marbella Boat Charter

Local knowledge matters when the day depends on the sea.

Marbella Boat Charter is based in Puerto Banús and coordinates private yacht charters, sailing, fishing, group events and yacht services around Marbella and the wider Costa del Sol. The strongest value is not simply access to boats; it is knowing how to turn a request into the right vessel, route and operational plan.
