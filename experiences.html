Experiences

Days on the water should be sold by atmosphere, not by menu labels.

The operator’s public offer already spans far more than “rent a boat”. A premium presentation treats each format as an experience: sunset charters, coastal escapes, fishing days, corporate programmes, group celebrations and longer routes toward Gibraltar or Morocco.

Sunset charter

Elegant, short and unforgettable.

For late-afternoon light, music on board and an easy sense of occasion. This is less about distance than it is about atmosphere and the right vessel.

Coastal escape

Swim, anchor and drift.

A polished charter for couples, families or friends who want sea time, swimming and a smooth premium pace without overcomplicating the day.

Fishing day

Specialist by nature.

Fishing deserves its own language and boat choice. It should feel purposeful and properly matched, not like an extra hidden in the catalogue.

Corporate charter

Larger groups still need polish.

For business hospitality, launches or social programmes where coordination, timing and guest movement all matter.

Celebrations

Private, social, elevated.

For birthdays, stag or hen formats and other group celebrations where the boat becomes part of the event identity itself.

Longer route

Gibraltar and beyond.

When the route is part of the statement, the day shifts from a short charter into a more ambitious yachting plan.

Support services

Catering and yacht management strengthen the whole brand.

Those services matter because they move the business beyond simple boat rental. They suggest a wider yachting presence, more operational depth and a more established luxury proposition.

How to brief the day

The useful input is simple: date, guests, style of day and route idea.

Once those four things are clear, the charter desk can usually recommend the right vessel very quickly.
