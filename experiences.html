Route atlas / service field

One coastline. Very different days.

MBC / CRS — route feasibility depends on vessel, weather, fuel and time

Puerto Banús is the base, not the boundary. The published operation covers Marbella, Estepona, Sotogrande and Benalmádena and advertises longer experiences toward Gibraltar and Morocco.

This page separates local cruising, long-range routes and activity formats so they are not flattened into identical “experience cards”.

Course sequence / 01–08

Choose the operating problem first.
